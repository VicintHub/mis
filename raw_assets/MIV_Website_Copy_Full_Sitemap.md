### Section — Our Story
**Eyebrow:** Our Story
**Heading:** A burden that began long before 2025.
**Body:** The story of MIV Strategic Initiative did not begin with any single event. It is the outworking of a burden God laid on the Founder of Men of Issachar Vision Inc., Rev. Samson Ajetomobi, many years ago — to intentionally influence strategic sectors of society beyond the four walls of the church.

What began as Leadership Development gatherings for legal practitioners, public office holders, and professionals grew, over time, into a dedicated strategic summit for senior Armed Forces and security officers — and from there, into a deliberate, sector-based strategy now expressed through four flagship programmes reaching academia, security, business, and traditional leadership.

Today, the Initiative exists to become a nationally recognised platform where government institutions, academia, business leaders, security agencies, development partners, and faith communities collaborate to build a more ethical, prosperous, and transformed society.

### Section — Mission / Vision / Mandate
**Mission:** To intentionally influence strategic sectors of society — academia, security, business, and governance — through Christ-centred transformation, ethical leadership development, and practical capacity building.

**Our Approach:** We deliver genuine professional value first — leadership insight, funding access, welfare support — so that participation is sought after, not merely tolerated. Character and conviction follow naturally, carried through testimony and example.

# DEDICATED SUB-PAGE 3 — Armed Forces & Uniformed Officers Outreach

### Hero
**Eyebrow:** Security & Uniformed Officers
**Headline:** Strengthening faith, discipline, and service in uniform.
**Subhead:** A dedicated outreach honouring the men and women of the armed and uniformed services — now extended into structured, verified welfare support for the families they leave behind.
**CTA:** [Partner With This Outreach →]

### Section — Why This Outreach Exists
**Heading:** Those who defend a nation deserve more than gratitude in passing.
**Body:** Officers across the armed and uniformed services carry the weight of national security, often at extraordinary personal cost. This outreach brings the ministry of the Word, discipleship, and leadership training into that world — strengthening faith, moral discipline, and commitment to justice among those who serve.

### Section — Fact Strip
`17` Security Agencies Represented (2025)  ·  `9` Agencies at the 1st Edition  ·  `400+` Officers, Most Recent Gathering  ·  `2025` Widows' Empowerment Project Launched

### Section — Growing Institutional Trust
**Body:** Participation has grown from 9 security agencies at the outreach's first edition to 17 agencies represented at its 2025 edition — nearly doubling institutional representation in a single cycle, across the Nigerian Army, Nigeria Police Force, NSCDC, FRSC, EFCC, Nigeria Customs, the Air Force, the Navy, and further uniformed corps.

### Section — Widows of Fallen Officers Support & Empowerment Project
**Heading:** Beyond honour — sustainable support.
**Body:** This year, we are taking a bold step to support widows of fallen officers across multiple security agencies — men and women who paid the ultimate price for our safety. Beyond financial support, selected widows undergo up to three months of vocational training (tailoring, catering, confectioneries, fascinator-making, caregiving, and knitting), empowering them for sustainable living.

Every nomination is verified through a formal interview process, and every intervention is monitored after the fact — a standard shaped by an early, hard-learned lesson in ensuring support reaches those who need it most, and stays with them.

# DEDICATED SUB-PAGE 4 — Royal Fathers' (Oba's) Conference

### Hero
**Eyebrow:** Traditional & Royal Leadership
**Headline:** Traditional rulers, set apart in worship and humility.
**Subhead:** A gathering unlike any council or coronation — where royal fathers set aside the symbols of title and earthly authority, to worship God and seek wisdom for the weight they carry.
**CTA:** [Partner With This Conference →]

### Section — Why This Conference Exists
**Heading:** Even a king needs a source of wisdom greater than his own.
**Body:** Nigeria's traditional institutions remain custodians of culture, land, and community cohesion — wielding a moral authority that often outlasts political administrations. The Royal Fathers' (Oba's) Conference creates a dignified, reverent space in which traditional rulers set aside the symbols of office to pray for grace to lead their people with integrity, peace, and compassion.

### Section — What Happens at the Conference
- **Corporate Worship** — royal fathers, in full regalia, lifting their voices together in a rare, unified expression of humility.
- **Prayer & Consecration** — dedicated time set aside for personal and communal prayer.
- **Teaching** — addressing godly leadership from a traditional ruler's unique vantage point.

### Section — A Different Kind of Room
**Body:** In a room usually defined by title, protocol, and hierarchy, this conference asks something rare: that every crown be set down, if only for a few hours, before God. Photographic records from past editions show royal fathers in postures of prayer, consecration, and quiet fellowship with one another — a picture of leadership grounded not in power, but in humility.
