Why This Outreach Exists

Those who defend a nation deserve more than gratitude in passing.

Officers across the armed and uniformed services carry the weight of national security, often at extraordinary personal cost. This outreach brings the ministry of the Word, discipleship, and leadership training into that world.

We work to strengthen faith, moral discipline, and commitment to justice among those who serve, ensuring they feel valued and supported by the community they protect.

Institutional Integration

Nearly double the institutional trust, in one cycle.

Participation has grown from 9 security agencies at the outreach's first edition to 17 agencies represented at its 2025 edition — nearly doubling institutional representation in a single cycle. We collaborate with leadership across army, law enforcement, and paramilitary organizations.

Widows' Support & Empowerment

Beyond honour — sustainable support.

This year, we are taking a bold step to support widows of fallen officers across multiple security agencies — men and women who paid the ultimate price for our safety. Beyond financial support, selected widows undergo up to three months of vocational training, empowering them for sustainable living.

Tailoring & Fashion Design
Catering & Confectioneries
Fascinator-Making & Crafts
Caregiving & Knitting

Rigorous Needs-Based Selection Process

Every nomination for the Widows' Empowerment Project is verified through a formal interview process, and every intervention is monitored after the fact. This standard was shaped by an early, hard-learned lesson in ensuring support reaches those who need it most, and stays with them as a sustainable livelihood.
