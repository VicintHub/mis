Security & Uniformed Officers

Nearly double the institutional trust, in one cycle.

Participation grew from 9 security agencies at the outreach's first edition to 17 agencies represented at its 2025 edition.

The Widows of Fallen Officers Support & Empowerment Project has since introduced an ongoing, verified form of welfare impact — needs-based support paired with up to three months of vocational training.

Academic Transformation

A combined reach of over 1,000, in a single maiden edition.

The Academic Summit & Banquet's first edition reached 200+ academic leaders in person and 800+ more via livestream.

A further 22 tertiary and specialised institutions across Oyo State have been formally mapped for ongoing leadership and curriculum gatekeeping engagement.

Business & Economic Development

Built to test, before it scales.

MBS 1.0 was deliberately convened in-house to strengthen execution and feedback channels before a planned public-facing MBS 2.0.

The pilot program was positioned before an exhibition audience of 1,500+ Christian entrepreneurs, investors, and professionals, establishing strong baseline metrics for commercial alignment.
