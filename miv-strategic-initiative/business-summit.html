Why This Summit Exists

Business is a calling, not just a career.

Entrepreneurship and enterprise are the engine of employment and national productivity. The MIV Business Summit exists to equip entrepreneurs, professionals, and business leaders with practical knowledge, mentorship, and funding opportunities.

We integrate Biblical principles of stewardship and commercial excellence, focusing on building businesses that generate employment and strengthen communities.

Summit Components

What happens at the Business Summit

01

Keynote & Panels

Strategic commercial insights from established corporate leaders, discussing ethics and integrity under market pressure.

02

Pitch Competition

A mentorship-driven pitching tournament connecting emerging entrepreneurs with investors, offering seed funding up to ₦1,000,000.

03

Vendor Exhibition

Positioning your brand and inventory directly before a highly motivated audience of over 1,500 Christian entrepreneurs and professionals.

04

Financial Masterclasses

Practical workshops focused on working capital, cash flow management, bank loan opportunities, and seed-funding compliance.

05

Prophetic Impartation

A dedicated session focusing on spiritual keys, ministerial covering, and purpose-driven corporate calling, ministered by Rev. (Mrs) Stella Ajetomobi.
